Service Terms

Standard service terms governing managed digital presence, cloud infrastructure, domain administration, and email security solutions delivered by Advantello.

Advantello Services

Advantello is a business division of Southbridge Enterprise Systems Limited providing enterprise digital presence management, including sovereign domain management, business email security, managed cloud hosting, setup, migration, and dedicated human engineering support.

Third-Party Cloud Infrastructure

Advantello coordinates with enterprise upstream infrastructure providers (such as Microsoft 365, ICANN-accredited domain registrars, and cloud data centers). Advantello operates as your single managed point of contact, ensuring security configurations meet enterprise benchmarks.

Domain Sovereignty & Legal Ownership Guarantee

When Advantello manages a client's corporate domain, the client remains the legal registrant at all times. Ownership records are protected and transparently maintained under client control.

Service Levels & Support

Commercial plans, uptime commitments, billing terms, acceptable use requirements, and support response times are governed by individual master service agreements executed with Southbridge Enterprise Systems Limited.
